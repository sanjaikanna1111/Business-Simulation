# WRSX Strategic Business Simulation

**Board-level decision making simulation for a global advertising and communications firm**

This project simulates the role of a Board Director at WRSX, a global advertising agency operating in New York, Paris, London, and Singapore. Across six board cycles, strategic decisions were made and evaluated with a focus on revenue growth, profitability, risk governance, and long-term competitive positioning.

---

## Project Overview

The simulation analyses the strategic development of WRSX over multiple board meetings. It evaluates both financial and non-financial performance and examines how key decisions influenced:

- Revenue growth
- Profitability
- Risk management
- Governance capability
- Long-term competitive advantage

The full strategic analysis is documented in the accompanying report:

**[WRSX REPORT_11.pdf](WRSX%20REPORT_11.pdf)**

| Metric                        | Outcome                                      |
|-------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Revenue                       | Grew from **£200m** to **£329.8m**           |

## Strategic Frameworks Applied

- **Porter’s Five Forces** – Industry attractiveness and competitive pressure analysis
- **Ansoff Matrix** – Growth strategy selection (market penetration, development, diversification)
- **Resource-Based View (RBV)** – Identification of internal capabilities and sources of sustained advantage
- **Organisational Design Theory** – Alignment of structure with strategy
- **Blue Ocean Strategy** – Exploration of uncontested market space and value innovation

---

## Key Strategic Insights

- **Over-expansion without proper sequencing** reduced short-term profitability and stretched operational capacity.
- **Growth must be aligned with governance capability** — rapid expansion without strengthening controls increases risk.
- **Talent investment** emerged as a critical driver of long-term differentiation and client retention.
- **Balanced integration** of new initiatives improved operational stability and supported sustainable performance recovery.
